## When you need a commercial license

You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- Deploy FlightCompass or a derivative as a SaaS, internal tool, or partner-facing product **without** publishing your modifications and integrations under AGPL.
- Bundle FlightCompass into a closed-source application or distribution.
- Remove the attribution requirements or embed the tool inside another branded product.
- Receive indemnification, a defined support SLA, or contractual warranties that AGPL explicitly disclaims.

You do **not** need a commercial license to:

- Use the public hosted demo at the URL listed in the README.
- Self-host an unmodified copy for personal or internal evaluation use.
- Fork the repository, modify it, and publish your fork under AGPL-3.0.
- Cite the project, methodology, or scoring approach in academic or editorial work.

## Terms

Commercial licenses are negotiated case by case. Typical structures include a one-time fee, an annual subscription, or a revenue-share arrangement, depending on deployment scale and use case. The license grants rights additional to AGPL — it does not remove the public AGPL release.
