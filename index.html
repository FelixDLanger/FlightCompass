About FlightCompass

A research-oriented strategy explorer, not a booking platform. Built by Felix Diego Langer as part of doctoral research in business administration (DBA) at GlobalNxt University.

Methodology: Flight prices are sourced from the Aviasales/Travelpayouts cache (refreshed weekly, up to 7 days stale). Award charts, cashback rates, and hotel benchmarks are hand-curated. The recommendation engine compares multiple booking strategies on a per-route basis using transparent scoring formulas.

Editorial principle: honest analysis first, monetization-aware second. Affiliate links are shown only when the corresponding option is the engine\u2019s best recommendation for the route. Where other paths score higher \u2014 direct airline booking, miles redemption, or hotel partner perks \u2014 the analysis surfaces those instead, with no affiliate revenue earned.

Contact: reach me on LinkedIn for questions, corrections, or research collaboration.

Data & privacy: no cookies, no tracking, no personal data collected by this page. Affiliate clicks redirect to external services with their own privacy policies.

Not financial advice. Not a booking tool. Always verify prices, award availability, cashback rates, and FHR perks on the actual booking site before transacting. Currency conversion, taxes, and fees may vary by channel.
