About FlightCompass

A research-oriented strategy explorer, not a booking platform. Built by Felix Diego Langer as part of doctoral research in business administration (DBA) at GlobalNxt University.

Methodology: Flight prices are sourced from the Aviasales/Travelpayouts cache (refreshed weekly, up to 7 days stale). Award prices follow each program's published chart where one exists and observed price levels where it does not (reviewed October 2026). Cashback rates and hotel benchmarks are hand-curated. The recommendation engine compares multiple booking strategies on a per-route basis using transparent scoring formulas.

Editorial principle: honest analysis first, monetization-aware second. Affiliate links never influence the scoring: every route is ranked the same way whether or not a link earns a commission. When another path scores higher, such as direct airline booking, a miles redemption, or hotel partner perks, the analysis says so, and no commission is earned on those paths. Affiliate links are labeled in the panel and disclosed in the footer.

Contact: reach me on LinkedIn for questions, corrections, or research collaboration.

Data & privacy: no cookies, no tracking, no personal data collected by this page. Affiliate clicks redirect to external services with their own privacy policies.

Not financial advice. Not a booking tool. Always verify prices, award availability, cashback rates, and FHR perks on the actual booking site before transacting. Currency conversion, taxes, and fees may vary by channel.
